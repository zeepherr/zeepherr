## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛒 SpecHub
Second-hand IT accessories marketplace platform designed to resolve pricing & condition trust issues in pre-owned tech trading.

**Highlights**
- Dual Buyer & Seller workflows with listing creation and atomic database order reservations
- AI-assisted product identification (Gemini) and automated market price research (Tavily)
- Platform-mediated physical hardware inspection workflow (PASS / REJECTED status handling)
- Stripe payment integration with automated refund handling on inspection failure
- Real-time chat and support case management via Socket.IO

**Tech**
`React` `Node.js` `Express` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `TanStack Query` `Zustand` `Socket.IO` `Stripe` `Gemini AI`

### 🏍️ HurngMoto
Motorcycle shop management system uniting POS checkout, product inventory, service cataloging, and customer membership tracking.

**Highlights**
- Point of Sale (POS) checkout supporting direct transactions & hold/pending orders
- Product inventory management with motorcycle brand & model associations
- Customer membership management and customer-motorcycle profile linkage
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across Admin, Staff, and Member roles
- Layered backend architecture with Zod schema validation and Prisma transaction safety

**Tech**
`React` `Node.js` `Express` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `TanStack Query` `Zustand` `Tailwind CSS` `JWT`
